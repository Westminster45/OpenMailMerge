# Code of conduct

OpenMailMerge welcomes anyone who wants to use it, report a problem or contribute.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, including when you disagree.
- Keep discussion on the subject of the issue or pull request.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or content.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Spam, trolling or deliberately derailing discussion.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, report the comment or account to GitHub with **Report content** (in the ··· menu on any comment) or **Block or report** (on the user's profile). For anything else, open an issue.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock discussions, or block anyone whose behaviour breaks this code.
